What can I do with RoxRec? (Competition, Pace, Training)
RoxRec has three modes. Competition records a full HYROX race with live RoxZone transition tracking. Pace Control takes a goal finish time and gives you per-run paces, station targets and live banked time (ahead/behind). Training covers interval timers (AMRAP / EMOM / Tabata / For Time), single-station practice, compromised-running drills, race simulations and periodized weekly plans. Pick a mode on the Apple Watch start screen.
How does RoxZone (transition) tracking work?
RoxRec tracks transitions with a two-tap model: tap to end a segment (the RoxZone clock starts as you jog to the next station), then tap to start the next segment. Each transition is recorded as its own split and graded elite / good / fair / slow, so the time you spend in the RoxZone is never lost inside a run.
How do heart-rate zones and the lactate-threshold test work?
RoxRec uses five lactate-threshold heart-rate zones. Run the optional guided LTHR field test to set your threshold (or let the app estimate it), then your heart rate shows in zone colors during a workout. A haptic buzzes your wrist when you spike into Z4 or Z5 so you can ease off without looking down.
Which devices are supported?
RoxRec runs on Apple Watch Series 7 and later with watchOS 10 or higher, paired with the iPhone app for history and analysis. The app is available in Korean and English. Make sure your devices are on the latest software.
How do I pause or stop a workout?
Press and hold the screen for 1.5 seconds to access the pause and stop options. The hold delay is intentional to prevent accidental stops during intense segments.
Where is my workout data saved?
All workouts are automatically saved to Apple Health (HealthKit) after you finish. You can also view your full history and analysis in the RoxRec iPhone app.
How do I create a photo record card?
After completing a workout, open the workout detail in the iPhone app. Tap the share icon to overlay your finish data on a photo from your camera roll. Save or share directly to Instagram or other apps.
Why isn't heart rate showing during my workout?
Make sure RoxRec has permission to access Health data. Go to iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → RoxRec and enable heart rate access. Also ensure the watch is worn snugly on your wrist.
Does it know my division's weights?
Yes. Set your division and gender in your athlete profile and each station card shows the correct weight, reps/distance and wall-ball target height for Open or Pro, women or men — used in both Competition and Training modes.
Does RoxRec support Double HYROX or team formats?
RoxRec currently supports the standard Singles HYROX format (8 runs × 8 stations with RoxZone transitions) across all three modes. Doubles and relay formats are planned for a future update.
What expert analytics does RoxRec provide?
On iPhone, RoxRec turns your sessions into about 21 sports-science metrics — pacing variability (CV), run-fade index, aerobic decoupling, training load and form (TRIMP, CTL/ATL/TSB), weakest-station ranking, per-station PB curves and plan-vs-actual. A plain-language coaching summary tells you what to focus on next. RoxRec v1.1 ships fully free; some advanced sections may be gated in a future update.
