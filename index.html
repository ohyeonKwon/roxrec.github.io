RoxZone two-tap tracking
Tap to end a segment, the RoxZone clock starts, tap to start the next. Transitions become a first-class split — graded elite / good / fair / slow — not a number you lose in the run.
🎯
Pace control & banked time
Enter a goal finish time. The app builds fatigue-adjusted run paces, 8 station targets and a RoxZone budget, then shows live banked time — ahead or behind — every lap of the race.
🏋️
Training modes
AMRAP / EMOM / Tabata / For Time intervals, single-station practice, compromised-running drills, full / half / quarter race simulations, and periodized weekly plans.
❤️
HR zones & lactate threshold
Five lactate-threshold zones with a guided LTHR field test and zone-color heart rate. A haptic buzzes your wrist the moment you spike into Z4 or Z5 — no need to look down.
📊
Expert analytics & coaching
~21 sports-science metrics — pacing CV, run-fade index, aerobic decoupling, training load and form (TRIMP / CTL / ATL / TSB), weakest-station ranking — distilled into a plain-language coaching summary.
📸
Photo card & HealthKit
Overlay your finish data on any photo and share it. Every session saves to Apple Health, with full history on iPhone and Apple Watch. Your data, always yours.
