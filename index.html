Why RoxRec

More than a recorder.

Record, pace and train — every transition, every split, every zone, captured and decoded.

01
RoxZone two-tap tracking
Tap to end a segment, the RoxZone clock starts; tap to start the next. Transitions become a first-class split — graded elite / good / fair, not a number you lose in the run.
02
Pace control & banked time
Enter a goal finish. RoxRec builds fatigue-adjusted run paces, 8 station targets and a RoxZone budget, then shows live banked time — ahead or behind — every lap.
03
Training modes
AMRAP / EMOM / Tabata / For Time intervals, single-station practice, compromised-running drills, full / half / quarter race simulations, and periodized weekly plans.
04
HR zones & lactate threshold
Five lactate-threshold zones from a guided LTHR field test, with zone-color heart rate. A haptic buzzes your wrist the moment you spike into Z4 or Z5 — no need to look down.
05
Expert analytics & coaching
~21 sports-science metrics — pacing CV, run-fade, aerobic decoupling, training load & form (TRIMP / CTL / ATL / TSB), weakest-station ranking — distilled into a plain-language coaching summary.
06
Photo card & HealthKit
Overlay your finish data on any photo and share it. Every session saves to Apple Health, with full history on iPhone and Apple Watch. Your data, always yours.
